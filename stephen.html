Biography

Dr Stephen Lee is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Technological University Dublin. He completed his PhD (2020-2024) in Advanced Manufacturing Technology at the University of Limerick, where his research focused on real-time digital twin and machine learning solutions for hole quality and tool condition monitoring in robotic drilling of composite materials. He holds a BSc in Aircraft Maintenance and Airworthiness Engineering from the University of Limerick/HAECO Xiamen Technical Training Centre (2015-2019).

During his doctoral studies, Stephen gained extensive experience in composite manufacturing, mechanical testing, and SEM analysis. He provided subject matter expertise on a 1.4M euro industrial robotic system, leading root cause analysis and preventive maintenance strategies. His most recent work developed a machine learning model for in-situ evaluation of drilling quality and tool condition, significantly reducing production time and improving cost efficiency in industrial settings. He has also contributed to academia through teaching and module coordination roles and has mentored MSc and BSc students in both laboratory and academic writing tasks.

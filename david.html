Biography

David Salter is a PhD researcher affiliated with the Thermo‑Fluids Research Group at TU Dublin. His current work focuses on thermodynamic and fluid dynamic optimization.

Research Interests

  • Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer
  • Multiphase Flow Systems

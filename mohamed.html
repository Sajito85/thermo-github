Biography

My research interests are at the intersection of fluid dynamics, structural vibrations, and renewable energy harvesting using computational simulations for solving engineering problems. I am especially interested in fluid-structure interaction (FSI) issues, where I formulate and use numerical models to investigate phenomena such as vortex shedding, wake dynamics, turbulence intensity and their effects on structural response. The long-term use of my basic research is in the field of renewable energy. I am concerned with bridging the gap between simulations and real-world design optimizations of energy systems like enhancing the aerodynamics of wind turbine blades or optimizing the energy harvesting of oscillating underwater systems—to further the viability and sustainability of clean energy solutions

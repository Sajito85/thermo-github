About the TFES Research Group

We advance thermo-fluids science for energy-efficient, resilient systems — from data-centre cooling and hybrid digital twins to micro-scale jet actuation and advanced manufacturing.

Mission & Focus

Our work bridges rigorous experimentation and high-fidelity simulation with real-time data and control. We target tangible impact: lower energy, higher reliability, and faster design cycles for thermal systems.

What we do

  • Physics-informed simulation of turbulent & multi-scale flows
  • Hybrid digital twins: CFD + reduced-order models + sensor data
  • Energy-efficient cooling hardware (air amplifiers, ejectors)
  • Experimental rigs, sensing, autonomy & control

Why it matters

  • Reduce energy & carbon in digital infrastructure
  • Improve system resilience & uptime
  • Accelerate design via real-time surrogate models
  • Bridge lab demonstrators to industrial deployment
